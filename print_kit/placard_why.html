Most wellness apps narrate at you. They sell you presence. They charge for stillness. They turn breath into a streak.

This work asks what is left when nothing is being sold. What you are when no one is watching. Who you are, to you.

A chair. A screen that sees your stillness. A headband that hears it. A cork wall that holds what you can finally put down. A small altar for the ones who stay.

Six minutes. One breath. A room built for one body at a time. Yours.
