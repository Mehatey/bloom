Build order — Friday 1pm to 6pm

1–2pm · the backbone

  • Mount 75" TV on rolling stand, eye-level when seated
  • Place chair 8-9 ft from screen
  • BrainBit station table to chair's right
  • Connect laptop · run ?show=1&noaudio=0 · test full journey end-to-end

2–3pm · the walls

  • A1 BLOOM logo hung above door
  • A2 placard at chest height beside door
  • Card bowl on plinth or shelf at entry
  • 3 question cards on cork wall
  • 200 cards + pens + pushpins in bowls

3–4pm · the layers

  • Blue fire glass — Fibonacci spiral on wall behind chair (silicone adhesive, one piece at a time, follow phyllotaxis template)
  • Mylar strips — hang 6-8 from ceiling above chair · twist gently
  • Fresnel lens — drill hole top edge · hang on monofilament at seated eye level · 4-5 ft from screen
  • Die-cut film overlay — hang between chair and screen, slightly angled

4–5pm · the altar

  • Turntable in back-right corner · cube on top · slow rotation (33⅓ rpm)
  • Two-way mirrors flanking · face inward to create ∞ illusion
  • Vision Pro on small stool beside
  • IMG_3825 marker printed and placed on altar surface for AR coda

5–6pm · the critique reveal

  • Red filter reveal sheets mounted on side wall
  • Red filter cards (3×5", red gel laminated to white card) in small bowl on entry table with note: "hold this up to the wall."
  • Final lighting check — single warm uplight on blue fire glass spiral
  • BrainBit + Kinect connection test with someone sitting

6pm · doors

  • Press begin. Bloom opens.
  • Stay near the BrainBit station — help visitors put it on
  • Refresh tab between visitors (idle screen shows automatically)
  • Refill cards bowl as needed

If something breaks

BrainBit doesn't connect

  • Run ?brain=force on the show URL — uses simulated signal
  • Visitor experience unchanged

Kinect doesn't connect

  • Show still runs — scenes default to ambient values
  • Loses lean / hand interactivity but story arc intact

Audio cuts out

  • Reload tab · scenes are visual-first
  • Backup: ?noaudio=1 for fully silent run

Stressed / overwhelmed

  • Walk to the cork wall. Read one card. Breathe.
  • Then come back.
