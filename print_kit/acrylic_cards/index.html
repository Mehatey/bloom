Acrylic mandala cards.

10 intricate cards · 3"×3" · for clear acrylic laser cut

Sheet recommendation: 12"×24" clear acrylic 1/8" or 1/16" thick. 8 cards fit per 12"×24" sheet at 3"×3" each. Two sheets cuts all 10.
