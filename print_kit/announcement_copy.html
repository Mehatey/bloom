05 · Press / DT 2026 page blurb

100 words · for the cohort site / press release
Bloom is a meditation that listens. The visitor sits in a chair while a BrainBit EEG senses their stillness, a Kinect notices their body, and a 75-inch screen unfolds fifteen procedurally-generated scenes across a six-minute journey tuned to their nervous system. The work opens with a satirical overstimulation collage ending in the popup "You Are Not Enough · Install" — and resolves into a cork wall where visitors write one card and pin it. The installation is both a real meditative experience and a critique of every wellness app that ever charged for stillness. Six minutes. One body at a time. Yours.

After a year of work, I'm thrilled to share that my MFA thesis at Parsons School of Design opens this week. Bloom is a meditation that listens — a body-responsive seated installation combining BrainBit EEG sensing, Microsoft Kinect, generative WebGL visuals, and image-tracked AR via Apple Vision Pro, all in service of one question: what is presence when nothing is being sold? The work both builds a real contemplative experience and critiques the wellness-app industry that has commodified silence into a subscription. Friday May 15 + Saturday May 16, 6pm · Room 1205, Parsons. Open to the public. Documentation at mehatey.github.io/bloom A huge thanks to my advisor Clarinda Mac Low and the entire DT 2026 cohort. #MFA #DesignTechnology #Parsons #Meditation #InteractiveInstallation
