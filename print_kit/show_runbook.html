Friday.

Show-day runbook · 12pm → 9pm · Room 1205

Morning — before you arrive

  • Charge laptop to 100%. The show runs from here.
  • Charge BrainBit fully. Bring charging cable.
  • Pack: HDMI cable, USB-C adapter, extension cord (6ft minimum), backup laptop charger.
  • Eat a real breakfast. You will forget to eat after 1pm.
  • Wear black. Move freely. Closed shoes.

1:00 — Arrive at Room 1205

1:00
Walk the room. Note power outlets, sightlines, where to hide cables. Decide chair orientation if not already locked.
1:15
Hang the A1 BLOOM logo above the door. Mount the WHY + HOW placards at chest height, one on each side of the entry.
1:30
Position the 75" TV. Eye level when seated. About 8 ft from chair. Run HDMI from laptop. Place the chair.
1:45
Set up BrainBit station table on the chair's right side. Lay the headband on a clean towel. Plug in charger.
2:00
Run a full end-to-end test. Open complete.html?show=1. Wear the BrainBit yourself. Sit in the chair. Confirm: overstim opens, transitions to meditation, all 15 scenes play, closing breath ring appears. If anything breaks, you have 4 hours to fix it.

3:00 — The walls + the altar

3:00
Blue fire glass spiral on the wall behind the chair. Use clear silicone. One piece at a time. Follow a Fibonacci spiral template. Takes ~45 min.
3:45
Mylar strips from ceiling above the chair. 6-8 strips. Twisted. Irregular heights.
4:00
Fresnel lens hanging at seated eye level. Drilled hole, monofilament from ceiling tile.
4:15
The altar — turntable in back-right corner. Glass cube on top. Two two-way mirrors flanking. Vision Pro on a small stool beside it. Place the printed AR marker on the altar surface.
4:45
Cork wall setup. Engraved plaque at top. 200 blank cards. Pens. Pushpins. Red filter cards in bowl at entry.

5:00 — Lighting + final pass

  • Dim room lights. Single warm uplight on blue fire glass.
  • Hang die-cut overlay between chair and TV (clear film, monofilament).
  • Mount red-filter reveal sheets on side wall.
  • Place sticker sheet of overstim notifications on door / entry wall.
  • Lay out: business cards bowl, thank-you cards, red filter cards.
  • Walk the room as a visitor would. Sit in the chair. Look around. Adjust whatever feels off.

5:45 — Final test + breathe

  • Restart laptop. Open complete.html?show=1. Confirm it plays on the TV.
  • Check BrainBit connection. Backup: ?brain=force
  • Check Kinect connection. Backup: scenes work without it.
  • Hand the attendant script to whoever is helping you.
  • Drink water. Sit on the chair yourself. Breathe for 6 minutes.

6 → 9pm.

Doors open · the show · the end of the night

6:00 — Doors open

6:00
Take the door sign off. Let people in. You are no longer the artist. You are the host.

Per visitor — the loop

~0:00
Greet. Smile. Let them read the placards. Don't rush.
~0:30
Seat them. Place BrainBit. Step back. Press begin.
~0:45
Flip the door sign to "IN SESSION". Stand to the side, look away, give privacy.
~6:45
When closing breath ring appears — wait for visitor to open eyes. Take your time. Help remove BrainBit.
~7:00
Point to cork wall + altar. Offer thank-you card. Flip door sign back to OPEN.
~7:30
Reset between visitors: wipe BrainBit forehead pad with alcohol swab (60 seconds). Refresh the show tab (idle screen returns). Total turnaround per visitor: 8-9 min.

Throughput math

  • ~6 min meditation + 2-3 min wrap = ~9 min per visitor
  • 3 hours = ~20 visitors maximum
  • Realistic with social time, breaks: ~15 visitors per night
  • Two nights: 30 visitors total

If a line forms

  • Tell people they can come back during the show window.
  • Hand out thank-you cards and business cards while they wait.
  • Let them write a card for the cork wall before their session — gives the wall extra cards.

9:00 — End of Friday

  • Photograph the cork wall — wide + close-ups before leaving.
  • Power down laptop. Charge BrainBit. Charge Vision Pro.
  • Note: scenes that hit hardest. Visitors who paused. Cards that surprised you.
  • Pack snacks for Saturday. Sleep.

Saturday — quick reset

  • Arrive 4pm. Power on. Run end-to-end test. Refill cards if needed.
  • 6pm doors open. Same loop.
  • 9pm: photograph FINAL cork wall. Take everything down or leave for Sunday.

The wrap (Sunday onward)

  • Save every card. They are the show's analog memory.
  • Send thank-you emails to attendees you know.
  • Post documentation: photo of wall + one card that hit you hardest.
  • Rest. You did the thing.

You have always been bloom.
