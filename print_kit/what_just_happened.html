what just happened

For six minutes, the room was reading you.

A small sensor on your forehead measured your stillness. A camera in the corner saw your body. The visuals you watched were tuned to your nervous system in real time.

The breath ring widened as you slowed. The mandala opened as you leaned. The lightning grew when you held your hand up.

Most wellness apps tell you what to do. This one listened.

it was always you.
