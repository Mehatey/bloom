MFA Design + Technology · Parsons · 2026

Bloom.

A meditation
that listens.

A seated installation. The body controls the room. The room responds with patterns. The room is also a critique of every wellness app you have ever been sold.

Sit in the chair. A BrainBit EEG senses your stillness. A Kinect notices your body. A 75-inch screen unfolds 15 generative scenes across a 6-minute meditation — nebulae, mandalas, sand, lightning, breath. The scenes are tuned to you.

Before the meditation, an overstimulation collage — news, ads, surveillance, AI weirdness — ending with the popup "You" Are Not Enough · Install. You watch Install get pressed. What you receive is Bloom: a wellness app that holds you.

After the meditation, write one card. Pin it to the wall. By Saturday night the wall is the show.
